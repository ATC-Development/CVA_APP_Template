Decision this screen forces
Acknowledgment is the fairness mechanism. A supervisor-authored account that travels up the chain with no employee response is the shape that causes problems in a dispute. Three options: (a) acknowledgment required before the record locks — highest integrity, but a non-responding employee stalls the record; (b) acknowledgment invited, auto-locks after N days with "not acknowledged" stamped — recommended; (c) no acknowledgment at all — lowest friction, weakest position. This prototype implements (b) with a 10-day window.
