Prototype design notes · decisions this screen forces
  • The timeline is a read model, not a record. Nothing is authored here — every entry is projected from its owning aggregate (CVA, PPM, SupervisorNote, OneOnOne, ActionItem, GrowthRequest). Switching hats re-runs the visibility predicate; it does not re-query different tables.
  • Supervisor notes do not travel up the chain. Barry sees fewer entries than Maria. That follows the locked note rule (current supervisor + HR only) — confirm this is still the intent now that a chain view exists.
  • PPM stays invisible to HR until Complete. The Aug 2026 PPM appears for HR only because it completed. An in-flight PPM would not appear at all in hat 4.
  • Action items inherit their source's gate. Items promoted from a PPM goal are hidden wherever the PPM is hidden. Standalone and one-on-one items are not gated.
  • Open question surfaced by hat 1: the employee sees a record with visible gaps. Do we show "3 entries are not visible to you" (current behaviour, honest) or show nothing at all (concealed)? This is an employment-practice call, not a UI preference.
